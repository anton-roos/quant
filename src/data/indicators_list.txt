# Technical Indicators for Market Forecasting
# Supports: Forex, Indices, Commodities, Crypto
# Automatically computed by src/data/processor.py

date - The specific date of the trading day or data point
close - The closing price of the instrument at the end of the trading day
YesterdayClose - The closing price from the previous trading day
YesterdayOpenLogR - Log return calculated from yesterday's opening price
YesterdayHighLogR - Log return calculated from yesterday's high price
YesterdayLowLogR - Log return calculated from yesterday's low price
YesterdayVolumeLogR - Log return calculated from yesterday's trading volume (0 for forex)
YesterdayCloseLogR - Log return calculated from yesterday's closing price
MA10 - 10-day moving average of the closing price
MA20 - 20-day moving average of the closing price
MA30 - 30-day moving average of the closing price
DayOfWeek - Numerical representation of the day of the week (0=Monday, 6=Sunday)
DayOfMonth - The day number within the month (1-31)
MonthNumber - The month number within the year (1-12)
EMA10 - 10-day exponential moving average, giving more weight to recent prices
EMA30 - 30-day exponential moving average, giving more weight to recent prices
RSI - Relative Strength Index, a momentum oscillator measuring overbought/oversold conditions (0-100)
MACD - Moving Average Convergence Divergence, a trend-following momentum indicator
MACD_Signal - The signal line for MACD, typically a 9-day EMA of the MACD line
BollingerUpper - Upper band of Bollinger Bands, showing potential resistance levels
BollingerLower - Lower band of Bollinger Bands, showing potential support levels
Volatility_10 - 10-day volatility measure, indicating price fluctuation intensity
Volatility_20 - 20-day volatility measure, indicating price fluctuation intensity
Volatility_30 - 30-day volatility measure, indicating price fluctuation intensity
OBV - On-Balance Volume, combining price and volume to show buying/selling pressure (0 for forex)
ZScore - Statistical measure showing how many standard deviations away from the 20-day mean
overnight_gap - Price difference between previous close and current open (%)
abnormal_vol - Z-score of volume vs 20-day average (0 for forex)
volatility_5d - 5-day realized volatility (annualized)
volatility_20d - 20-day realized volatility (annualized)
momentum_5d - 5-day price momentum (% return)
momentum_20d - 20-day price momentum (% return)
skew_5d - 5-day price skewness, measuring asymmetry in return distribution
intraday_range - The difference between the day's high and low prices (normalized by close)
ADX - Average Directional Index (14-day), measures trend strength regardless of direction (0-100)
Stoch_K - Stochastic %K oscillator (14-day), overbought/oversold momentum indicator (0-100)
Stoch_D - Stochastic %D (3-day SMA of %K), signal line for stochastic crossovers
vol_ratio_5_20 - Ratio of 5-day to 20-day volatility; >1 signals short-term vol spike / regime shift
CLV - Close Location Value, where price closed within the day's range (-1 to +1), buying/selling pressure proxy
corr_SP500_20d - 20-day rolling correlation of instrument returns with S&P 500 (dynamic risk-on/off)
corr_SP500_60d - 60-day rolling correlation of instrument returns with S&P 500 (longer-term regime)

# Notes:
# - Volume indicators (OBV, abnormal_vol) are set to 0 for forex pairs (volume not meaningful)
# - All other indicators work across all asset classes (Forex, Indices, Commodities, Crypto)
# - Date is preserved in processed files for time-series ordering
